## Why write about Net Energy Metering (NEM)?

Take a look at this bill from Southern California Edison.  Notice anything confusing?

<img src="images/nem_sce_electric_bill.jpg" width=700>

The amount due is $34.32, but the year-to-date charges are $212.29.  Which amount should you pay?  

On the invoice, it says *Understand Net Energy Metering (NEM) billing*.  Then, it explains that you are billed at the end of the year for the net difference between the amount of electricity purchased and the amount your solar panels generated and sold back.  

There is more to the story.  Notice that the bill is 8 pages long.   Scrolling down you will find another section on page 5.

<img src="images/nem_sce_page_5.jpg" width=700>

Page 5 details delivery charges.  The energy charge total is $155.80 for the current month and $56.49 for the previous month resulting a total year-to-date energy charge of $212.29.

<img src="images/nem_sce_page_6.jpg" width=700>

The next page details supply generation by Desert Community Energy.  The *Additional Information* section on the right states that the NEM balance owed is $98.72, but on the left, the Your New Charges are zero.

In simple terms, Desert Community Energy generates wind and solar energy and Southern California Edison charges a fee to deliver it.
The year-to-date total for energy generation is $98.72 and the year-to-date total to delivery the energy to your home is $212.29.

The amount due each month is for SCE maintenance and operation.  At the end of the fiscal year in May, the total of both generation and delivery will be offset by the total electricity produced by your home solar panels and sold back to SCE.  In order to budget for this yearly bill, keep track of the year-to-date totals.

<img src="images/nem_green_button.jpg" width=700>

SCE participates in the [Green Button initiative](https://www.energy.gov/data/green-button) that provides utility customers with easy and secure access to their energy usage information.  Click on the  `Green button download data` to download usage information in csv format.
