## Why write about Net Energy Metering (NEM)?

Take a look at this bill from Southern California Edison.  Notice anything confusing?

<img src="images/sce_electric_bill.jpg" width=700>

The Amount due is $34.32, but the Year-to-date charges are $212.29.  Which amount should I pay?  

Right on the invoice, it says *Understand Net Energy Metering (NEM) billing*.  Then, it explains that you are billed at the end of the year for the net difference between the amount of electricity that you purchased and the amount your solar panels generated and sold back to SCE.  The amount due each month is for maintenance and operation.

There is more to the story.  Notice that the bill is 8 pages long.   Scrolling down you will find another section.
